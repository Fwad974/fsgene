Who We Are

FSGene Medical Molecular Genetics Laboratory in Jeddah works with the backing and know-how of FSG Lab Dubai.

Its work spans five connected areas: clinical molecular diagnostics, genomic and genetic testing, clinical training, biotechnology and clinical trial research. At the center are molecular diagnostic tests, screening for inherited disease and cancer, genome sequencing and specialist genetic analysis, all reported with an interpretation a clinician can use.

The laboratory also teaches. Its training programs and practical learning routes build technical skill in molecular and genomic science, and its R&D work keeps improving diagnostic methods, applied genetics and translational biotechnology.

On the biotechnology side, FSGene is adding genomic monitoring and is developing and manufacturing its own molecular and diagnostic kits for use in precision medicine, clinical labs and research.

All of this runs in a modern Jeddah laboratory where environments are controlled and workflows validated, which is what makes the results precise, dependable and well reported.

About FSG Lab

The First Stem Cell and Genomics Laboratory in Dubai works where precision science, advanced technology and clinical excellence meet, with a focus on stem cell science, genomic innovation and advanced biotechnology.

The laboratory holds GMP certification and CAP and AABB accreditation. Its services span stem cell science, genomics, molecular diagnostics, genetic testing, clinical training, biotechnology and clinical trial research, backed by its own research and development.

Its biotechnology division makes advanced biological products in ISO 5 cleanrooms under strict sterility and quality control, including stem cell-based products, CAR-T cell technologies, exosomes and regenerative medicine solutions. Based at the Golden Mile on Palm Jumeirah, it runs a hybrid model that combines family and public stem cell banking.

FSG Lab also offers stem cell banking designed for mothers alongside a wide portfolio of genetic tests, helping healthcare professionals choose the right test to guide patient care.

Non-Invasive Prenatal Testing/Screening (NIPT/NIPS)

Early, safe genetic insight into your baby's health. NIPT, also called non-invasive prenatal screening (NIPS), reads fetal DNA circulating in the mother's blood. It can pick up chromosomal conditions from about the tenth week of pregnancy and carries no risk for the baby.

Book a Consultation Quick Connect

Conditions Detected by NIPT/NIPS

  • Trisomy 21 (Down Syndrome)
  • Trisomy 18 (Edwards Syndrome)
  • Trisomy 13 (Patau Syndrome)
  • Sex Chromosome Abnormalities

Early, accurate insight for parents

Early Detection

Screens for conditions such as Down, Edwards and Patau syndromes from as early as week 10 of pregnancy.

Safe Procedure

A blood sample from the mother is all it takes, with no risk to mother or baby.

High Accuracy

More than 99% accurate in identifying chromosomal abnormalities.

Less Invasive

Fewer women need procedures such as amniocentesis or chorionic villus sampling (CVS).

How Does NIPT/NIPS Work?

  1. 1
    Blood CollectionA small blood sample is drawn from the mother, usually between weeks 10 and 14 of pregnancy.
  2. 2
    DNA AnalysisThe lab analyzes fetal DNA in the sample for extra or missing chromosomes.
  3. 3
    ResultsYour healthcare provider receives a detailed report that sets out any findings clearly.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is non invasive prenatal test?

A simple blood test, also known as non-invasive prenatal screening, that analyzes fetal DNA in the mother's blood. It can find conditions such as Down, Edwards and Patau syndromes early, with no risk to the baby.

How much does NIPT cost in Dubai?

In Dubai the test usually costs between AED 1,800 and AED 3,000, depending on whether screening for additional genetic conditions is included. FSG runs the test in house, with full quality control.

What is the NIPT test for blood types?

NIPT is mainly for chromosomal abnormalities, but some packages also report the baby's blood type, including Rh factor, which helps manage Rh incompatibility during pregnancy.

What is the NIPT test for a girl?

The test can show the baby's sex early by looking for X and Y chromosomes in the mother's blood. If only X chromosomes are found, the baby is female.
